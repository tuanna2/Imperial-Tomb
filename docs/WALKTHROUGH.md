# Imperial Tomb 0.2 — walkthrough (spoilers)

The chapter now has six rooms, 25 closeups, eight interactive mechanisms and five item recipes. The design target is **40–70 minutes for a first play without solutions**. This is a pacing target, not a measured playtest result. A player following this walkthrough will be much faster.

## Controls

Tap an object in the room to enter its closeup. The hand/eye button reveals all touch targets when necessary. Each target has an accessible label and a 50 logical-pixel touch area. In closeup, tap an inventory object and then **DÙNG…** to apply it. Hold an inventory object or use its info button to examine it. To combine: select the first item, tap **GHÉP**, tap the second. A wrong combination does not destroy anything.

Round dials advance one notch per tap. **VẬN HÀNH** tests the complete configuration. The pipe board tests actual connectivity. Half-adjusted mechanisms autosave; leaving a closeup does not reset them. Journal entries retain discovered clues. Hints have an indirect tier and a solution tier.

## 1. The false tomb

1. In **TIỀN ĐIỆN**, take the oil lamp and inspect the stele.
2. The oath orders **Sun → Mountain → Moon**. The stele also says Fleet II carried **two** torches.
3. Tap the three symbols on the stone lock in that order. Collect the reusable bronze stylus.
4. In **TÀNG THƯ**, take the brush, rice paper and charcoal from the conservation desk.
5. Read the ledger: Fleet I carried **four** lights; Fleet III carried **one**. Combine with the stele to set the chest to **421**. Take the jade seal and decree. The seal unlocks **TINH ĐIỆN**.
6. Select the brush and use it on the black mural. Then select the lamp and use it there again. The revealed route is **Moon → River → Sun**.
7. Use the stylus on the narrow letter compartment. Read Mai's letter; it records the final pressure levels: **old river 3, settling basin 1, counterflow 2**.

## 2. Mai's waterproof box

8. Enter **GIẾNG TRỜI** and take the rope from the bench.
9. Inspect the lotus relief: roots south, bud east, stem north, leaf west. Press **South → East → North → West** to retrieve an iron hook.
10. Combine **rope + hook**. Use the hooked rope at the well to retrieve a sealed tin.
11. Combine **sealed tin + stylus**. The reusable stylus remains in inventory; the tin yields a brass key and dry cloth. The key unlocks **CÔNG PHÒNG**.

## 3. The engineer's rules

12. Read the engineering plan in **CÔNG PHÒNG**. Mirror rings: **outer 2, middle 4, inner 1**, counted clockwise from the top of eight notches.
13. The balance cabinet has three constraints: middle = left + right; right = left + 1; sum = 10. Solve **left 2, middle 5, right 3**. Take the wheel handle and wrench.
14. Use the wrench on the pump cover. Inspect the open drawer for the pipe route.
15. Rotate the six pipe tiles so water enters the **left of the top-left tile**, travels right across the top row, down at the right edge, left across the bottom row, and exits **below the bottom-left tile**. Every pipe must connect with no leaks. Press **THỬ DÒNG NƯỚC**. The pump drains the bridge and unlocks **THỦY MÔN**.

Pipe orientations, if needed (clockwise quarter-turns from the built-in base shapes): top row **0, 0, 2**; bottom row **1, 0, 3**. Straight tiles also work after a half turn.

## 4. Reading the erased map

16. In **TINH ĐIỆN**, use the stylus to take the tarnished mirror off its wall bracket.
17. Combine **dry cloth + oil lamp** to make an oiled cloth. The lamp remains usable.
18. Combine **oiled cloth + tarnished mirror** to produce a polished mirror.
19. Use the polished mirror on the empty reflector stand. Set the three rings to **2, 4, 1**. Light reveals the erased map and releases the orrery safety catch.
20. Inspect the canal map. The main gate points toward three villages; the auxiliary line goes to the old riverbed. Use **rice paper and charcoal** on the map to obtain the village-name rubbing.
21. Use the bronze stylus on the orrery axis. Set the three rings to **Moon → River → Sun** from the cleaned mural. Collect the valve core.
22. Combine **valve core + wheel handle** to build a working valve wheel.

## 5. The river's return

23. Enter **THỦY MÔN**, inspect the overcarved plaque, and avoid the main release lever.
24. Use the complete wheel on the side valve. Set **old river 3, settling basin 1, counterflow 2** and operate it.
25. Leave through the maintenance tunnel carrying **the decree, Mai's letter and village-name rubbing**, having read the illuminated map and diverted the water. This produces the **true ending**.

## Other endings

- **Bad:** confirm pulling the main release before diverting the water.
- **Ordinary:** confirm leaving the maintenance tunnel before completing the diversion and all evidence requirements.
- Both irreversible choices have a confirmation dialog. A completed journey cannot grant further items or change its ending. Starting over asks before replacing the saved game.

## Saves

Save schema 2 retains inventory, knowledge, installed tools, journal and partial dial/pipe positions. Schema 1 journeys migrate without deleting acquired items or notes. An old completed ending remains completed. New evidence and machine requirements apply to unfinished journeys.
