# Imperial Tomb — Official solution (spoilers)

Estimated proof-of-concept duration: 10–20 minutes depending on exploration.

1. In TIỀN ĐIỆN take the oil lamp and read the stone stele. It says Sun → Mountain → Moon, and that Fleet II carried TWO torches.
2. On the three-symbol lock, tap Sun, Mountain, Moon. Take the bronze stylus.
3. In TÀNG THƯ read the ledger. Fleet I had FOUR lamps and Fleet III had ONE. Together with the stele the three-digit chest code is 421.
4. Open the jade chest with 421. Collect the jade seal and the imperial decree.
5. Select the oil lamp from inventory, then tap the dark mural. Read Moon → River → Sun.
6. Select the bronze stylus, then tap the narrow alcove. Collect Mai's letter.
7. Enter TINH ĐIỆN. Read the canal map (required for true ending). The main outlet points towards three villages; the auxiliary outlet routes back to the old river.
8. Tap the orrery with the bronze stylus in your inventory; solve Moon → River → Sun. Receive the copper valve wheel.
9. Enter THỦY MÔN using the jade seal. Do NOT open the main sluice.
10. Select the valve wheel, tap the secondary valve, and redirect the flood.
11. Tap the service exit for the TRUE ending, provided you read the map and collected both pieces of evidence.

Alternatives:
- Opening the main sluice before redirecting the flow leads to the BAD ending.
- Taking the service exit without completing the true-ending requirements leads to the ORDINARY ending.
- Continue, hints and notebook are available in the interface. Progress saves automatically.
